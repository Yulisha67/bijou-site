A template is a starting point. We tailor it to your celebration: colours, texts, sections and details.

  1. Colours and palette

    We change the colours to match your palette, flowers or dress code.

  2. Fonts and styling

    Different fonts, ornaments and illustrations in the same style.

  3. Add sections

    Your story, photos, transfer, seating, wishlist, planner contacts.

  4. Remove or reorder

    We hide sections you do not need or change their order.

    Greeting for guests, day programme, venues, your own photos.

    A version in Ukrainian or another language for your guests.
