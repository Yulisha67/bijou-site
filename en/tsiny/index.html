How long does the link work?

12 months after the event. After that we can extend it or keep the invitation as an archive.

How many rounds of edits are included?

One with Template, two to three with a bespoke wedding (Individual). An extra round costs $30, and date or address changes are always free.

Does the price depend on the number of guests?

No. You can invite as many guests as you like.

What if the details change?

We’ll update the invitation for free and the link stays the same.

How long does it take?

Template — 3 working days, Individual wedding — from 10, Individual event — 5–7, PDF — 3–5. We count from the day we receive your texts and photos. 48-hour express is +50%.
